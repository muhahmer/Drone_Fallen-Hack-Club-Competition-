# The Racing Drone
> Total Cost to Manufacture: 827 USD

## About My Project

#### What Is My Project? 
Designed for Fallen Hack Club Competition this Project is meant to surpass commercial drone's capabilities. Being made up of similar components GPS, gyroscopes, motor drivers this drone is capable of things that most commercial drones are unable to do such as RTH (return to home function) transmit high frequency video feed, give live stats on drone's battery and more. While also having codable functions this drone also holds capabilities (expected) to go at much faster speeds with a thrust to weight ratio of 4:1. In addition this drone also brings innovation to the marked with the single handed screen remote. Many high end companies combine their single hand controller with over expensive VRs where as this project has developed a way that a single hand controller integrated with a screen (non VR) using servos to tilt the screen according to the hand movement when controlling the drone.

#### Why I Made This?
The Reason I made this goes back many years to when I was a young kid (around 6 years old) when I first aspired to make my own drone. I made designs clueless on how it would work just driven by the love of drones. Later when I started getting into Arduino, which opened doors for me, I understood how real drones would work but still unable to make my own. As this competition has come up it has given me the opportunity to fill my childhood dream and make my very own custom drone, built by my own hands and designed by my own hands.

### Extra Details and Budget Justification 
The receiver that is being used in the project can also be used as a transmitter when libraries are added. The receiver has full capability to work as a transmitter and will work seamlessly with the rest of the sytem.

#### Expenses Justification

PCB Expenses  - PCB soldering is required due to the fact this project involves chips where they are very delicate. Personally I am not good at soldering and I don't want this reason to mess up the entire project. 

Shipping cost - Their is high shipping cost due to the fact that the purchases are being made from multiple sources. The reason purchases are being made from different manufactures are: not every seller has every seller has the exact component in their store and because this project requires                          authentic parts. When buying parts it is vital that they are authentic to prevent waste of money or loss of component capability. I have had experience when I bought components of Temu, which were clones, and they came out as really bad clones. The chips did not work properly, I had                   to download excess amount of libraries to try and make it work and it glitched alot 

3D printing   - Their is a medium high amount of 3D printing cost. This is not due to the filament, the filament chosen for this project has been the cheapest it can be while maintaining a high standard. For the drone the filament being used is Carbon fiber due to its properties: it is light weight,                  strong and impact resistant. Using this filament prevents drone damage in test conditions.
